Stock Exchange MarketFlux is a Business competition at PROCOM focused on real-time intraday trading simulation using PSX-listed stocks. Participants trade within a constrained time window, testing market timing, risk management, capital allocation, and decision-making under pressure. Teams consist of 1–2 participants. The competition follows a live market simulation format with no real money involved. Use of mobile phones, internet browsing, external tools, communication outside the team, or platform manipulation is strictly prohibited. Participants must remain at assigned systems and follow all organizing committee decisions.Rulebook for stockexchange: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1e2FgZCG6xAGVmopH45pFSJJZ3Lc9tAky/view
