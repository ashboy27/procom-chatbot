DataVerse is a Business competition at PROCOM focused on financial analysis, fraud detection, and risk assessment. Teams of 1–3 participants analyze provided datasets to identify fraud, evaluate financial performance using ratios, and present insights through interactive Excel or Power BI dashboards. Judging is based on analytical accuracy, insight quality, dashboard effectiveness, and clarity of recommendations; external data, AI tools, plagiarism, or late submissions result in disqualification.
