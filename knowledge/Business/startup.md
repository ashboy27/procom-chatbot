Startup Showdown is a Business competition at PROCOM focused on entrepreneurial pitching and startup ideation. Teams of 2–5 participants present innovative business ideas or early-stage ventures to industry experts and judges through structured pitch rounds. Evaluation is based on creativity, problem–solution fit, market opportunity, business model strength, scalability, and presentation clarity. Plagiarism, false information, unethical conduct, or missing scheduled pitches results in disqualification. Pitch decks are limited to 10 slides, judges’ decisions are final, and participants must bring their own laptops and pitch materials.Rulebook for startup showdown: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xYzmwFdHAyeSxScnlBIFkFql7jf_3xTQ/view
