Speed Soldering is an Electrical (EE) competition that evaluates electronics assembly skills across three rounds: an MCQ-based theory round, a software simulation round, and a final hardware soldering round. Held on 11th February from 10:00AM - 3:00 PM Eliminations occur progressively (30% then 50%), and winners are judged on accuracy and task completion time.Rulebook for speed soldering: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xYcZzLwPQjbbD6XoNIEbTsJCPuIpw0tJ/view
