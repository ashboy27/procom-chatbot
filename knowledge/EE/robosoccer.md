Robo Soccer is an Electrical (EE) competition where teams compete in one-on-one robotic football matches, aiming to score goals while defending their own side. Matches follow a knockout format with 50% elimination per round, and any draw is decided by a golden goal, testing robotic control, strategy, and real-time decision making.
