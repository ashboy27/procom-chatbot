Robo Sumo is an Electrical (EE) competition in which autonomous or remote-controlled robots battle to push their opponent out of a defined arena. Held on 11th February from 2:00PM - 4:00 PM The event uses a knockout structure with 50% elimination each round, and golden goal rules for draws, emphasizing mechanical strength, stability, and control.Rulebook for robo sumo: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XCoPDqBvzEwlnzDM3xSXIhV5Q0QDuvks/view
