Line Following Robot (LFR) is an Electrical (EE) competition where fully autonomous robots must follow a predefined line through curves and obstacles in the shortest possible time. The competition is conducted in one-on-one knockout rounds with 50% elimination per round and golden goal tie-breakers, testing precision and sensor integration.
