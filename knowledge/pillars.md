Pillars of PROCOM’26
Food Fest
A lively array of food stalls invites participants to unwind, connect with friends, and recharge for the next adventure. The Food Fest adds a social dimension to the event, making networking informal and enjoyable alongside the technical activities.
Competitions
Over 50 thrilling national competitions await participants, spanning fields such as computer science, artificial intelligence, FinTech, engineering, business, e-gaming, and more. These contests challenge skills, encourage innovation, and recognize top performers with prizes and accolades.
Startup Showdown
Ambitious young entrepreneurs take the stage to pitch daring and innovative ideas. Judges and investors evaluate presentations, providing guidance, mentorship, and potential funding opportunities, creating a vibrant platform for budding startups.
Tech Expo
The Tech Expo offers hands-on experiences with cutting-edge products, live demonstrations, and B2B networking opportunities. Participants can explore the latest innovations from top tech companies and rising startups, gaining insight into industry trends and future technologies.
Career Fair
PROCOM’26 connects students directly with top employers. Attendees can submit resumes, participate in interviews, and explore hiring and internship opportunities, bridging the gap between academic learning and industry careers.
