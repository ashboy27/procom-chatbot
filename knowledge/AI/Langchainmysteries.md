LangChain Mysteries is a hands-on Artificial Intelligence competition focused on agentic AI and LangChain pipelines. Participants solve a digital investigation using Speech-to-Text, document analysis, and multi-step reasoning chains. They are evaluated on building LangChain-based pipelines, applying Speech-to-Text for audio intelligence, performing structured document analysis using LLMs, implementing multi-step reasoning, and designing modular, readable AI workflows. The competition has three stages: Stage 1 Audio Intelligence (analyze audio files), Stage 2 Document Forensics (analyze multi-page document dossiers), and Stage 3 Final Reasoning (combine insights from previous stages). Each stage has equal weightage, and additional marks are awarded for code quality, modularity, and logical reasoning. Conducted on a custom web platform; code is submitted for each stage. Target audience: Agentic AI/LangChain enthusiasts. Level: Intermediate/Expert. Duration: 3 hours 9:00AM–12:00PM 11th February 2026. Team size: 1–3 members. AI usage: All AI agents allowed. Rounds: 1 round, 3 stages. Yes participant have to bring own laptop. RULEBOOK must be referred for rules and evaluation.
