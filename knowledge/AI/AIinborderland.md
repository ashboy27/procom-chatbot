Artificial Intelligence AI Competition
AI in Borderland is a practical, problem-solving AI competition testing coding, analytical skills, teamwork, and time management under pressure. It covers Data Science (EDA, calculations, feature extraction, basic model implementation, no visualization), Deep Learning, Computer Vision (matplotlib, pandas, numpy, OpenCV, PIL), and Recommendation Systems. Each domain has equal weightage. The competition provides 4 questions per domain at basic to intermediate difficulty. Conducted on a custom web-based platform, teams can choose domains and questions strategically. Target audience: College and university students. Level: Intermediate. Duration: 3 hours 9:00AM-12:00PM 11th February 2026. Team size: 1-3 members. AI usage: Not allowed, documentation and cheatsheet provided. Rounds: 1 round, 4 questions. Laptop required: No. RULEBOOK must be referred for rules, evaluation, and sample questions.Rulebook for AI in BorderLand: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1SK0A4kbIwCowKlCbvm4se_Yt61LjZNIY/view
