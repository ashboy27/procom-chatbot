The event takes place on 11–12 February 2026. Individual competitions have specific timings listed in the schedule. For example, CS competitions run mainly from 9:00–16:00, EE competitions from 10:00–16:00, and general competitions have their own times as per registration.
Where will the competitions be held?
All competitions will take place at FAST NUCES Karachi, with specific labs or venues assigned for each category. Participants will be informed of their assigned locations on arrival.
Registration & Eligibility
How do I register for a competition?
Registration is through the official event portal. Each competition may have separate registration requirements and fees.
Can I participate in more than one competition?
Yes, but participants must manage timing conflicts. Some competitions may require exclusive participation (e.g., timed rounds).
Are there team limits?
Team sizes vary by competition: Business competitions (1–5), CS competitions (1–3), EE competitions (1–3), general competitions may be individual or team-based as specified.
Competition Rules
Is internet access allowed?
It depends on the competition. Most CS and CTF competitions do not allow external internet usage except for specific platforms like VJudge or GitHub. Business competitions allow no external tools or internet browsing. General and EE competitions may allow internet for documentation only if mentioned.
Are mobile phones allowed?
Mobile phones are strictly prohibited during competitions unless explicitly stated. Phones may need to be submitted to coordinators.
