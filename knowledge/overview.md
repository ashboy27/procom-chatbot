Event Overview and what PROCOM is
PROCOM’26 is Pakistan’s premier annual technology festival, hosted by FAST National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (FAST‑NUCES), Karachi.
It brings together students, professionals, and innovators from across the country to compete, create, and connect through technology and innovation.
The event has been running since 1998, making it one of the longest‑standing tech events in Pakistan.
PROCOM’26 provides a platform for technical excellence, creative problem solving, and industry-academia engagement.
PROCOM includes competitions, workshops, seminars, and networking opportunities designed to help participants sharpen skills and meet professionals
