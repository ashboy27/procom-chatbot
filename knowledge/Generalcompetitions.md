There are 8 exciting modules in General Category of PROCOM
CSI (General Competition) tests deductive reasoning as participants analyze clues and solve a crime scenario under time pressure.
Scavenger Hunt (General Competition) is a high-speed campus challenge where teams crack riddles, navigate locations, and race against rivals.
Human Ludo (General Competition) is a life-sized strategy game combining planning, chance, and player interaction.
Chess (General Competition) is a pure strategy contest focused on foresight, patience, and tactical precision.
Glyphs of Atlantis (General Competition) is a card-based strategy game involving riddles, teamwork, and adaptive decision-making.
Filmmaking (General Competition) requires teams to create a short visual narrative, judged on creativity and execution.
Photography (General Competition) challenges participants to capture a single themed shot evaluated on composition and storytelling.
Reel Making (General Competition) focuses on creating impactful short-form video content with strong engagement.
