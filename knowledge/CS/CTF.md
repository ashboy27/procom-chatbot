Capture the Flag (CTF) is a Computer Science competition set in ShadowVerse under the Static Echo Protocol. The event runs from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM and follows a Jeopardy-style format using intentionally vulnerable systems. Participants analyze compromised services, applications, binaries, or datasets to locate and submit hidden flags. Teams may consist of 1 to 3 members. The use of fuzzing, brute-force attacks, sharing flags or solutions, interfering with other teams, or external AI tools is strictly prohibited. Participants must bring their own laptops along with chargers and required adapters. Seats are limited. Stable internet will be provided, though a personal hotspot is recommended.Participants must bring their own laptop and chargers.
