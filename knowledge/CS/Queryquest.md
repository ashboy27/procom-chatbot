Query Quest is a Computer Science competition set in the DataVerse, held on 11th February running from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM. It is a three-hour, single-round SQL-based data integrity challenge where teams analyze, query, and repair corrupted datasets to stabilize failing timelines. Teams may consist of 1 to 3 members. Supported SQL dialects are MySQL and Oracle SQL. Internet access, mobile devices, and external AI tools are strictly prohibited, and collaboration with other teams will result in disqualification. Partial submissions are allowed and scored accordingly. Organizers reserve the right to modify rules in case of technical issues.Personal laptops are not allowed. The Query Quest rulebook is available at: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ys27njHS86o4ssp4cECHfWwlxfsR3f-P/view
