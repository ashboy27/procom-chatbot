Incognito Investigation is a Computer Science competition from Obsidiverse, held from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. This one-day cyber mystery has two rounds: Digital Hunt and Interrogation Chamber, where teams of 1–3 members solve puzzles, analyze clues, and identify the culprit. Evaluation is based on accuracy, supporting evidence, time, and logical reasoning. AI use, collaboration with other teams, sabotage, or external assistance is prohibited. Organizers may modify rules. Personal laptops are not allowed. Incognito Investigation rulebook is available at: https://drive.google.com/file/d/16AfGjueLIoZeSjhk-HO_BgxW0Kc910jm/view
