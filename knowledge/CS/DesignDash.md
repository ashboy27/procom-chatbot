Design Dash is a Computer Science competition from PixelVerse, held on 11th February from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. This four-hour, time-bound UI/UX challenge requires teams of 1–3 participants to design and adapt app or site interfaces in response to dynamic Reality Shift Challenges. Teams create 3–5 screens per event using Figma, Adobe XD, or Canva, with unique universes, users, constraints, and visual styles. AI tools, plagiarism, pre-made UI kits, online references, collaboration with other teams, and mobile use are prohibited.Participants have to bring their own laptop. Judges’ decisions are final. The Design Dash rulebook is available at: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1olVE8kHi34aiybLcQDcdGU-JSFtF2clI/view
