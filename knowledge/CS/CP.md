Competitive Programming (CP) is a Computer Science competition in PhantomVerse, held from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM. This 2.5-hour, ICPC-style algorithmic contest involves debugging and solving problems under time pressure for teams of 1–3 participants on VJudge. Scoring considers submission time plus penalties for wrong attempts. Internet access is allowed only for submissions, and mobile phones must be submitted to coordinators. Collaboration with other teams is prohibited. Allowed languages are C, C++, Java, and Python. Plagiarism, misconduct, or misuse of internet resources results in disqualification.Personal laptops are not allowed.
