CodeSprint is a Computer Science competition in CodeVerse, held on 11th February from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM for teams of 1–3 participants. This multi-round coding contest involves restoring stability in CodeVerse through algorithmic challenges. PCs are provided; allowed languages are C, C++, Python, and Java. The three rounds are: Fragmented Logic Recovery – a warm-up to restore broken logic, Failing Time Flow – solving problems under strict time pressure, Core Logic Confrontation – the final round with wrong-submission penalties for advanced algorithmic problems. Topics include basic math, implementation, searching/sorting, and programming fundamentals. No pre-written code, external internet, AI tools, or outside collaboration is allowed. c.
