Debug Duel is a Computer Science competition in Paradox-Verse, held from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM for teams of 2–3 participants. This timed debugging contest challenges participants to resolve syntax, logic, and pattern-based errors across three rounds: 1) Confrontation – 20% weight, 2) Counter Manifestation – 40% weight, and 3) The Awakening – 40% weight, with each round lasting 45 minutes. Languages allowed are Python, Java, C, and C++. Internet use is prohibited, though hard copies of hints/resources may be provided. Collaboration across teams, mobile devices, and edibles are prohibited. Partial submissions are scored proportionally. Plagiarism, misconduct, or unauthorized internet use leads to disqualification.
